Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the service, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so we can show it back to you.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve Spendly: to display your expenses and summaries, to authenticate your account, and to communicate with you about your account when necessary.

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.
  • We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.
  • We only access your data to provide support or maintain the service.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only process data as necessary to support Spendly's operation and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account support channel.
